# Artwork generation
Built-in image_gen used. Existing approved dark concepts were edited separately to remove all typography while retaining their right-hand sculptures, black background and 3:1 composition. Profile preserves cobalt ring, prismatic ribbon, chrome sphere and layered glass. ZOLA preserves eye, five orbital bodies and instrument lines. Klack preserves exploded mechanical switches, amber cap, chrome knob and waveform.

Memory Reel: new text-free panoramic 3:1 black artwork. Left 46% empty. Right: curved photographic film frames with mountains, sea, forest and road, amber celluloid edges, warm projector beam and restrained waveform. Original illustrative scenery; not personal footage. No text, logos, people or fake UI.

Finals are assembled from these separate text-free raster layers and font-based HTML typography. Individual objects inside each artwork remain raster imagery, not editable 3D models. Fonts and licenses are retained alongside the source.
